# Art credits and licensing

Every image here is a derivative work: an Unsplash photograph rendered through
the ASCII Magic engine (`characters` style). Source photos are listed with the
licence they were used under.

**Unsplash License** — verified at https://unsplash.com/license on 2026-11-18:

> "Commercial and non-commercial purposes" — "No permission needed (though
> attribution is appreciated!)"

Attribution is not required. It is given here anyway, because the photographers
earned it and because a licence trail should exist in the repo rather than in
someone's memory.

The licence does restrict one thing: *"Images cannot be sold without significant
modification."* We do not sell the images; they are site decoration, and the
ASCII rendering is itself a significant modification.

URL and licence here at the same time, or the next person has no way to prove
